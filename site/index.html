Luck or skill?

The gap between points and xPTS describes what happened, not why. Over one season a gap this size is mostly noise: the analysis behind this dashboard found that a club's first-half gap barely carries into its second half, and that the clubs furthest ahead give up almost all of it.

xPTS is summed over the matches that have one. Where a club's MP and its xPTS coverage differ, the gap is still like-for-like: the points side counts only those same matches.
