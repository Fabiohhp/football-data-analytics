On the finishing column

npG − npxG is how many more non-penalty goals a player scored than their chances were worth. It is the most tempting column here and the least predictive: the analysis behind this dashboard found it barely repeats from one season to the next, while shot volume repeats strongly. Read it as a description of the season that happened, not a forecast of the next one.
