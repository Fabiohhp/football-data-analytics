Reading the map

Coordinates are Understat's, normalised to a 0–1 pitch and drawn here without rescaling — the same orientation the notebooks use. Every penalty therefore lands on exactly one point, which is the check that confirms the axes are the right way round.

Own goals are shown with the shots because that is how Understat records them: they are a shot result, on the team of the player who scored them. They are never counted as goals for this club.
